The Two Series

Core Series

Motherboard, circuitry, custom-PC-lighting energy. Neon on dark metal, radial symmetry, engineered precision. Labels in all-caps Kaushan Script with a hollow embossed outline in icy silver-cyan, typeset deterministically, never AI-redrawn.

Pastel Series

Lighter tones, candy textures, whimsy. Nebula blooms and smoke instead of circuits, the collection’s soft counterweight to Core’s machinery. Labels in the same hand, in warm cream.

About this gallery

SPITE was named in November 2026, born from the anti-AI “theft” debates, and launched publicly on Threads as @circuit_research. Copyright infringement, plagiarism, and theft are three distinct things; this collection is the standing argument that a human aimed the machine and the work is his.

Only watermarked posting versions live here. The clean 3840 × 3840 masters stay private, and any future editions will be announced by the artist, nobody else. The full index lives in COLLECTION.md, and the edition research in NFT-ROUTES.md.
