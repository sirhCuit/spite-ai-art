Core Series Origin Series

This debut series of mandalas is the core of SPITE. All other works came after, and revolve around it, and for that it earned half its name.

The rest of the name was earned via capturing the mystery of raw, powerful technology as is seen at the center of the mothership hinted at in the Uploaded rendering.

Other examples of inspiration include a computer’s CPU as its core, and we can’t forget clean, sprawling circuitry found in all kinds of devices.

Pastel Series Origin Series

The second series in SPITE, the pastel series lives far off in space invoking ethereal vibes, and calming wonder in viewers.

Featuring whimsical designs, and airy palettes, the mandalas in this series aim to capture brand new cosmic events that no human has ever seen. One of those being the unmistakable Nova Bloom.
